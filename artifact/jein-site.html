Chapter 01 · The starting point

Who I am, and what J.E.I.N. connects

idea → system → project → value

J.E.I.N.—Joshua Elekwa Interactive Network—is where my work in business, technology and entrepreneurship comes together in one place.

The idea is simple. Most practical problems sit between departments: between a good offer and the operations that deliver it, or between a useful tool and the people who need to adopt it. I work in that gap, connecting business thinking, digital systems and hands-on execution.

This site follows that path in six chapters: who I am, how I think, what I build, what I have learned, how I contribute, and where I am going.

  • Business
  • Technology
  • Systems
  • Execution

Background

  • Studied Supply Chain Management and Mathematics (Data Science), with hands-on work in digital marketing and e-commerce.
  • Part of the Mount Royal University Institute for Innovation and Entrepreneurship ecosystem and its Makers Lab.
  • Has lived and worked in four countries across Africa and North America.
  • Approaches social problems through social innovation and human-centred design.

Privacy notice · draft for review · updated 18 October 2026

How your contact details are used

What I collect

Only what you type into the introduction form: your name, role, organisation and industry if you give them, your topic and answers, your email address, and any LinkedIn or website link you add. No phone number is needed.

Why

To read your message and reply to you about it. Your answers are not used for advertising, and you will not be added to any mailing list.

Where it goes

Submissions are stored by the platform hosting this site, sent to Joshua’s email inbox, and kept as a contact record in his CRM (HubSpot) so conversations can be followed up. Analytics, where enabled, only count steps and never receive your name, email or message.

How long, and your choices

Records are kept while a conversation is active and reviewed at least once a year. You can ask for your details to be corrected or deleted at any time by sending a new introduction with “Something else” and saying so.
