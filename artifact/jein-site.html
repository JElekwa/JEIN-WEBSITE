Chapter 01 · The starting point

Who I am, and what J.E.I.N. connects

idea → system → project → value

I'm a business-minded technology enthusiast, entrepreneur, and lifelong learner interested in how organizations use technology, systems, and people to solve problems and create value.

My experience spans e-commerce, sales, digital marketing, business development, operations, website development, and community involvement. I've built and managed digital storefronts, developed websites, experimented with automation and business tools, participated in international business competitions, and worked on initiatives focused on entrepreneurship and social impact.

I'm developing my capabilities in information technology and information systems, with a longer-term interest in business transformation, technology consulting, business systems analysis, and quality and process improvement.

I want to work on problems where commercial understanding and technical execution reinforce one another: helping organizations evaluate their needs, improve their processes, implement useful systems, and deliver better outcomes.

This website is a record of that journey—a portfolio of projects, experiments, professional experiences, and lessons learned.

How I Approach Problems

I start with how the business actually runs, before choosing any tool.

  1. 01
    Understand how the business runs

    Where value is created, who is involved, and what the work depends on.

  2. 02
    Map the process

    Lay out the steps and requirements as they really happen today.

  3. 03
    Find where value leaks

    Spot the manual workarounds, delays and hand-offs that cost the most.

  4. 04
    Then choose the tools

    Pick the process change or system that would matter most, and implement it in a structured way.

        Privacy notice · draft for review · updated 19 October 2026

        How your contact details are used

        What I collect

        Only what you type into the introduction form: your name, role, organisation and industry if you give them, your topic and answers, your email address, and any LinkedIn or website link you add. No phone number is needed.

        Why

        To read your message and reply to you about it. Your answers are not used for advertising, and you will not be added to any mailing list.

        Where it goes

        Submissions are stored by the platform hosting this site, sent to Joshua’s email inbox, and kept as a contact record in his CRM (HubSpot) so conversations can be followed up.

        Anonymous site statistics

        The site can count anonymous events: contact-form steps, which project pages are opened, which outside links are followed, and clicks on podcast, community-join and résumé links. These events never include your name, email or message. No analytics tool is installed yet; if one is added, this notice will name it and say what it stores.

        How long, and your choices

        Records are kept while a conversation is active and reviewed at least once a year. You can ask for your details to be corrected or deleted at any time by sending a new introduction with “Something else” and saying so.

        Recognition · verified only

        Awards and Recognition

        Awards, placements and recognition that can be verified. Team results are credited to the team.
